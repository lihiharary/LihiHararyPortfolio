Back-Up: Salesforce Companion

Smart Performance Support for Cognyte

1. Organization & The Challenge

Client: Cognyte (NASDAQ: CGNT). Context: A complex B2G sales environment with long sales cycles.

The Problem: Although reps were trained in MEDDPICC, they failed to apply it within Salesforce. The CRM was perceived as a 'policing' burden, leading to poor data quality and forcing management to rely on guesswork.

2. The Solution: Back-Up

Instead of traditional training, we developed a smart performance-support overlay that transforms Salesforce into a proactive sales companion.

The Sales Rep’s Perspective (Efficiency & Trust):
  • Pre-Meeting: A 'Smart Cheat-Sheet' with critical questions tailored to the stage.
  • During Meeting: Distraction-free interface to focus on the conversation.
  • AI Copilot: Listens, analyzes, and auto-fills data. Rep simply reviews and approves, removing grunt work.
  • Data Control: Rep chooses which notes stay private and which outcomes to publish.
The Manager’s Perspective (Control & Visibility):
  • Instant Visualization: 'Color Wheels' show deal health instantly, highlighting missing data.
  • Smart Gates: Identifies Red status (missing criteria) and blocks premature advancement.
  • Insights: Analyzes bottlenecks for reliable forecasting.
3. The Process

Our breakthrough came from a mindset shift: from 'Enforcement' to 'Performance Support'.

  • User Research: Identified Salesforce was seen as a policing tool.
  • Safe Space: Architectural separation between private drafts and public view.
  • Modular Development: Scalable from Logic Wizard to advanced AI Copilot.

Pasta Maker Companion

Smart Interface for Culinary Equipment

Architecture & Prototyping

Built from scratch in Figma, starting with information architecture through to a fully interactive prototype. The goal was to simulate the end-to-end user journey while reducing click-fatigue and visual cognitive load.

Culinary UI Language

We established a minimalist, elegant design concept. Choosing a delicate line-art illustration style paired with a meticulous palette of deep burgundy on a light cream background, creating high contrast and maximum readability for users while working in a kitchen environment.

Generative AI Integration

The interface's graphic assets (machine illustrations, discs, and raw materials) were generated using AI image generators. Through precise prompt engineering, we controlled the 2D style and maintained a completely uniform visual language across all Figma screens, avoiding any disruptive realistic elements.

Growing & Caring for Plants

Interactive Module for Children

About the Project

This interactive e-learning designed specifically for young children to introduce the three essential conditions for plant growth: soil, water, and sunlight.

The learning experience is intuitive and exploratory, allowing children to actively engage with the content rather than passively consume it. Through colorful animations and simple feedback mechanics, learners reveal each condition and advance to a knowledge-check quiz.

Design & Technology

All visual elements—illustrations, icons, and layout—were fully created in Adobe Illustrator, using a clean, playful, and age-appropriate vector style.

The module was built in Articulate Storyline 360, combining instructional design principles with custom visual storytelling.
