Back-Up: Salesforce Companion

Smart Performance Support for Cognyte

1. Organization & The Challenge

Client: Cognyte (NASDAQ: CGNT). Context: A complex B2G sales environment with long cycles.

The Problem: Despite MEDDPICC training, sales reps viewed Salesforce as a bureaucratic burden ("policing tool"), leading to poor data quality and forcing management to rely on guesswork for forecasting.

2. The Solution: Back-Up

Instead of traditional training, we created a smart performance-support overlay that turns Salesforce into a proactive companion.

The Sales Representative’s Perspective (Efficiency & Trust):
  • Pre-Meeting: A "Smart Cheat-Sheet" with tailored critical questions.
  • During Meeting: Distraction-free interface to focus on the conversation.
  • AI Copilot: Listens, analyzes, and auto-populates CRM fields. Removes manual grunt work.
  • Data Control: Clear separation between private notes and public/managerial data.
The Manager’s Perspective (Control & Visibility):
  • Instant Visualization: "Color Wheels" show deal health at a glance (identifying missing data).
  • Smart Gates: Identifies Red status (missing criteria) and prevents premature advancement.
  • Insights: Analyzes bottlenecks for reliable forecasting.
3. The Process

We shifted the mindset from "Enforcement" to "Performance Support". Key elements included:

  • User Research: Identifying the friction points in the current workflow.
  • "Safe Space" Principle: Architectural separation between drafts and public view to build trust.
  • Modular Development: Scalable from Logic Wizard to advanced AI Copilot.

Pastella

Concept & UX Process

SYNAPSE

From Organizational Documents to Interactive Learning Presentations

Product Design · UX/UI · Multi-Agent AI · Instructional Design

About the Project

SYNAPSE helps instructional designers turn complex organizational information into structured learning experiences. We designed and developed a platform that coordinates specialized AI agents to analyze source materials, define learning objectives, structure content, and generate interactive presentations.

Approach & System Design

Drawing on the ADDIE instructional design model, we translated the instructional design process into a collaborative workflow between users and AI.

We defined specialized agents for research, learning architecture, content creation, interface design, and quality review, along with the coordination logic connecting their work.

Human-in-the-Loop checkpoints allow users to clarify requirements, review the learning blueprint, and approve content before presentation generation. The workflow was designed around source materials and explicit user input.

User Experience

We designed an integrated workspace combining conversational guidance, brand settings, and deliverable previews.

The central UX challenge was making a complex AI process understandable and manageable. We mapped the journey from document upload to final output, then used usability testing to refine instructions, interface terminology, and readability.

Tools & Technologies

Python · Streamlit · Ollama · Local Language Models · HTML · CSS

The platform runs locally, meeting the project’s deployment requirements.

Outcome

A working prototype that takes users from source documents and clarification conversations to an approved learning blueprint and an interactive HTML presentation.

The project brings together product thinking, instructional design, UX/UI, and AI development in a single guided workflow.
